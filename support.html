Frequently asked questions

Where is my data stored?

Your journal is stored on your device, and Tracedays has no servers. Tracedays goes online to look up weather and place names: your location rounded to about 1 km (and your device's IP address) goes to Open-Meteo for the weather, and locations or place searches go to Apple's Maps services to suggest place names. iOS may also download your photos from iCloud Photos, sync backups you keep in a cloud folder you choose (such as iCloud Drive), and include the app's data in iCloud Backup if you use it. See the Privacy Policy for details.

How do I delete my data?

Delete individual journal entries from within the app, or use Settings → Delete All Data. That removes all journal entries and any leftover places, events and tags; all photo copies saved by the app; every backup file in the app's own backup folder (including files from earlier installations or other devices); set-aside copies of damaged data; leftover temporary export files; cached weather responses; and the app's record of your last backup. If you chose a backup folder, you can separately choose to delete this installation's backups there (if the folder cannot be reached, the app tells you and deletes nothing there). It does not remove other backups in a folder you chose, files you exported, copies you stored elsewhere, device backups such as iCloud Backup, links to photos in your Photos library (the photos stay in Photos), the app's preferences, some internal app settings, or data iOS keeps for the app. Afterwards the app forgets its backup ID, so backups kept in your chosen folder are treated as an earlier installation's and are not overwritten or rotated; the folder stays selected, and new backups are saved there once your journal changes again (for example, when the next day's entry is created). Entries cannot be recovered in the app unless you restore from a backup you kept. Deleting the app makes iOS remove the app's own storage; it does not remove a backup folder you chose, exported files or device backups. See the Privacy Policy, Section 7.

Why does an AI-generated entry look wrong?

AI-generated journal entries are a creative starting point, not a factual record — they may misinterpret data or contain inaccuracies. See the Terms of Service, Section 4, for details.

How do I change what data Tracedays can access?

Open Settings within the app to toggle each data source on or off at any time.
