Frequently asked questions

Where is my data stored?

Entirely on your device. Tracedays does not operate servers and does not collect or transmit your personal data. See the Privacy Policy for details.

How do I delete my data?

Delete individual journal entries from within the app, or use Settings → Delete All Data to remove everything. Deleting the app itself also removes all locally stored data.

Why does an AI-generated entry look wrong?

AI-generated journal entries are a creative starting point, not a factual record — they may misinterpret data or contain inaccuracies. See the Terms of Service, Section 3, for details.

How do I change what data Tracedays can access?

Open Settings within the app to toggle each data source on or off at any time.
