1. Overview and Our Core Commitment

Tracedays is a private, on-device journaling app. Our foundational design principle is that your personal data never leaves your device under our control. We do not operate servers. We do not collect your data. We do not sell, share, or transmit your personal information to any third party.

This Privacy Policy describes what data Tracedays accesses on your device to provide its features, how that data is processed (entirely locally), and what rights you have.

2. Data We Access But Do Not Collect

Tracedays requests permission to access the following data on your device. This data is read locally to generate your journal entries. It is never transmitted to our servers because we do not operate servers. It is never shared with third parties.

Health & Activity Data (HealthKit)

Tracedays requests access to step count, active energy, exercise minutes, heart rate, and other activity metrics you choose to share. This data is accessed locally, processed on your device to contribute to your journal entry, and never leaves your device under our control. Tracedays does not use HealthKit data for advertising, marketing, or any purpose other than generating your personal journal entries. Tracedays does not store HealthKit data in iCloud.

Location Data

Tracedays requests access to your precise location to identify meaningful places in your day (e.g., "spent the afternoon at Dolores Park"). Location data is processed entirely on-device. You will receive an explicit confirmation prompt before any location-derived place information appears in your journal. Tracedays does not maintain a history of your location on any server.

Photos

Tracedays requests access to your photo library to identify photos taken during the day and include visual context in your journal entries. Photos are processed on-device. Photo content, thumbnails, and metadata are never transmitted externally.

Calendar & Reminders

Tracedays requests access to your calendar events and reminders to provide context for your day (e.g., meetings attended, tasks completed). This data is read locally and never transmitted.

Weather Data

Tracedays retrieves current weather conditions using the Open-Meteo API, a free weather service that does not require account creation. The only data transmitted to Open-Meteo is your approximate location coordinates (latitude/longitude) to retrieve a weather forecast. Open-Meteo does not receive any other personal information. Open-Meteo's privacy policy is available at open-meteo.com/en/terms.

3. AI-Generated Journal Entries and Apple's Foundation Models

When you use Tracedays's AI journal generation feature, your day's aggregated data (health metrics, location summary, calendar events, photo context, and weather) is processed by Apple's Foundation Models framework to generate a narrative journal entry.

On-Device Processing: For simpler requests, all AI inference occurs locally on your device using Apple's on-device Foundation Model. No data is transmitted anywhere.

Apple Private Cloud Compute (PCC): For more complex journal generation, data may be sent to Apple's Private Cloud Compute infrastructure. Apple's PCC uses end-to-end encryption and hardware-isolated enclaves. Apple has stated that data processed through PCC is not stored after processing and is not accessible to Apple employees or third parties. This processing is governed by Apple's privacy policy, not ours.

We do not send your data to any AI provider other than through Apple's Foundation Models framework. We do not use OpenAI, Google Gemini (directly), Anthropic, or any other third-party AI API.

4. Data Stored on Your Device

All journal entries, preferences, and app data are stored locally on your device using SwiftData. This data is under your control. Tracedays does not back up your journal data to our servers. If you have iCloud Backup enabled on your device, your device backup (which may include app data) is governed by Apple's iCloud terms and privacy policy. We do not control or access iCloud backups.

5. What We Do Not Do

  • We do not sell your personal information
  • We do not share your personal information with advertisers
  • We do not use your data for behavioral profiling or targeted advertising
  • We do not transmit your health data to any third party
  • We do not collect analytics about your app usage
  • We do not use crash reporting services that transmit personal data
  • We do not embed third-party SDKs that collect data
  • We do not maintain user accounts
  • We do not store any of your data on external servers

6. Children's Privacy

Tracedays is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are under 13, please do not use this app. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information through this app, please contact us and we will take steps to delete that information.

7. Your Rights and Control

Because your data is stored locally on your device, you exercise full control:

  • Access: Your data is in the app on your device
  • Deletion: Delete the app to remove all locally stored data. Use in-app settings to delete individual journal entries
  • Correction: Edit journal entries directly in the app
  • Portability: Not currently available — Tracedays does not yet offer a data export feature.

California residents: Because we do not collect or sell personal information, most CCPA rights do not activate in the traditional sense. Your data is on your device, not with us. If you have questions, contact us at the address below.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. If we make material changes, we will update the effective date and, where appropriate, notify you through the app. Continued use of Tracedays after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
