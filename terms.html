5. Health Data

The App reads your steps, walking and running distance, exercise minutes and workouts from Apple HealthKit, only if you allow it, solely to show them in your journal and to describe your day in general terms. It only reads; it never writes to Health. We do not share HealthKit data with third parties. We do not use HealthKit data for advertising or marketing. We do not send HealthKit data off your device. The App does not include Health figures or workouts in backups or exports, although the journal text and tags in a backup may mention the activity level or the kind of workout in general terms. Consistent with Apple's guidelines, HealthKit data will not be used for any purpose other than providing the App's journaling features to you directly.
